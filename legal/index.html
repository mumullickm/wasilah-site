Privacy

Privacy first

Wasilah does not require an account, on this website or in the app. We never sell your data, and nothing from your prayers or your reading ever leaves your phone.

  • No account or sign-in required to use the site or the app.
  • The app uses your location only to work out prayer times and the Qibla, on your device.
  • Anonymous analytics (Google Analytics) help us improve the site. Your IP is anonymised.
  • The Android app sends Meta an install and app-open signal, so we can tell whether our ad campaigns reach people who actually use it. Nothing from your worship goes with it, and nothing at all on iPhone. Section 3.6 has the detail.
  • Email us anytime to ask what data we hold or to request deletion.
